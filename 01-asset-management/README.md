# Asset Management

## Objective

The purpose of this section is to identify and classify the
information assets of a fictional public-sector organization.

## Organization

The assessment uses a fictional organization called the
Passport and Immigration Organization (PIO).

PIO processes passport, immigration and residency-related
information and relies on information systems to deliver
its services.

## Asset Management Approach

The assets are assessed according to:

- Confidentiality
- Integrity
- Availability
- Business Criticality

Each classification is supported by a short rationale explaining
the potential impact of loss, unauthorized access, modification
or unavailability.

## Scope

The initial asset inventory covers:

- Information systems
- Databases
- Identity and access systems
- Network infrastructure
- Endpoints
- Security systems
- Backup systems
- Documents and data
- Communication systems

## Deliverable

The main deliverable for this section is the:

**Asset Register**

The asset register provides a structured overview of the
organization's key information assets and their security
requirements.
